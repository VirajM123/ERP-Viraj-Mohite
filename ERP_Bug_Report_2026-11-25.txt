1) FIRMS MASTER: Edit, Update, and Delete Not Working
   - Files:
     * src/Dashboard.jsx (saveFirm: L16396-16458, fetch at L16414; editFirm: L16491-16515; deleteFirm: L16517-16556)
     * backend/server.js (PUT /api/firms/:id: L27981-28027; DELETE /api/firms/:id: L28030-28049; GET /api/firms: L7209-7375)
   - Root Cause:
     1. saveFirm and deleteFirm use raw fetch(...) instead of secureFetch(...), bypassing CSRF/auth headers required by requireRoles("DISTRIBUTOR_ADMIN").
     2. editFirm only sets setEditFirmId(firm._id) without fallback firm._id || firm.id.
     3. PUT /api/firms/:id and DELETE /api/firms/:id filter by { _id: id, distributorId: req.auth.distributorId, firmId: req.auth.firmId }, whereas GET /api/firms returns all firms under distributorId. Updating or deleting any firm other than the currently active firmId fails with 404 "Firm not found".
   - Fix:
     * Switch saveFirm and deleteFirm to secureFetch.
     * Use firm._id || firm.id in editFirm.
     * In backend PUT/DELETE /api/firms/:id, scope by { _id: id, distributorId: req.auth.distributorId } (since DISTRIBUTOR_ADMIN manages all firms under their distributorId).
