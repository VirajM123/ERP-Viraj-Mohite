CUSTOMER-FACING ERP REPORT CATALOGUE
Updated: 17 November 2026

Purpose
This is a practical report menu for customers using the ERP day to day. It lists
the reports that should be available under each main report section. Status is
based on report names found in src/Dashboard.jsx and src/Report.jsx; a listed
report is not proof that its data, calculations, filters, or export have been
fully validated.

Status key
[EXISTS]  Listed in the current report catalogue or report definitions.
[CHECK]   Defined in report metadata, but dashboard catalogue visibility needs
          confirmation.
[ADD]     Recommended customer-facing report not found in the current catalogue.

REPORTS TO REVIEW FOR OVERLAP
- Product Ledger and Stock Movement Ledger both show stock movements. Keep both
  if Product Ledger focuses on a selected product's running balance and Stock
  Movement Ledger is the full source-document audit history.
- Current Stock and As On Date Stock share similar columns. Keep both if the
  latter reconstructs stock accurately as of a historical date.
- Party Wise Sales and All Party Wise Sales, and Product Wise Sales and All
  Product Wise Sales, are useful detail and consolidated versions when their
  selection and totals differ clearly.
- Company Wise Purchase and Company/Bill Wise Purchase can coexist as summary
  and bill-detail reports. Company Wise Purchase is now reachable from the
  Dashboard report catalogue.
- Stock And Sales Report belongs under both Sales and Inventory in the customer
  report menu because it combines sales and stock information.
- Credit Control Report is customer-facing and should be under Customer /
  Receivables. It may also be cross-listed under Control.
- Dashboard Reconciliation is an administrative diagnostic, not a routine
  customer accounting report. Keep it in the separate Administration section.

CUSTOMER REPORT MENU RECOMMENDATION
Show the main headings in this order:
Sales | Purchase | Inventory and Stock | Customers and Receivables |
Suppliers and Payables | GST and Tax | Financial and Accounting |
Banking and Cheques | Distribution and Delivery | Product and Master Data

Within each heading, display the [EXISTS] reports and any [ADD] reports that the
business has implemented and validated. Keep [CHECK] entries hidden until their
catalogue access is confirmed. Do not display a report as ready merely because
its name exists in source code; verify its filters, totals, permissions, export,
and date behavior before enabling it for customers.

IMPLEMENTATION STATUS
The lists below describe application code paths, not verified results from a
customer's live database. "Implemented" means a catalogue entry and report
generation path exist. Live database values, permissions, filters, and exports
have not been end-to-end checked; do not treat these reports as reconciled
accounting outputs until they are tested against the target firm.

IMPLEMENTED REPORT GENERATION PATHS
Sales:
- Party Wise Sales; All Party Wise Sales; Product Wise Sales; All Product Wise
  Sales; Stock And Sales.
- Sales Summary; Sales Register / Invoice Register; Sales Return / Credit Note
  Register; Sales Return Analysis by Product and Customer; Salesman Wise Sales;
  Area / Route Wise Sales; Company Wise Sales; Daily Sales; Monthly Sales
  Comparison; Top Selling Products; Counter Sales Summary.

Purchase:
- Company Wise Purchase; Company/Bill Wise Purchase; Product Wise Purchase.
- Purchase Summary; Purchase Register / Supplier Invoice Register; Purchase
  Return / Debit Note Register; Purchase Return Analysis by Product and
  Supplier; Supplier Wise Purchase; Purchase by Company and Supplier; Purchase
  Rate / Last Purchase Rate.

Inventory and stock:
- Current Stock; As On Date Stock; Product Ledger; Stock Movement Ledger;
  Stock And Sales; Damage Stock.
- Stock Valuation; Godown Wise Stock; Batch Wise Stock; Near-Expiry Stock;
  Expired Stock; Reorder / Minimum Stock; Stock Adjustment Register; Batch and
  Expiry Master List.

Customers and receivables:
- Party Outstanding; Customer Ageing; Customer Statement of Account; Bill Wise
  Outstanding; Collection Register; Collection by Salesman; Receipt Allocation
  and Unallocated Receipt; Credit Control.
- Customer Advance / Unadjusted Receipt is only represented by the unallocated
  entries in Receipt Allocation; it is not a separate report.

Suppliers and payables:
- Supplier Outstanding; Bill Wise Payables; Supplier Statement of Account;
  Supplier Debit Note Register.

GST, finance, banking, distribution, masters, and administration:
- Sales GST Register; Purchase GST Register; CRN GST Register; GST Summary;
  GSTR1; E-Invoice Register and IRN Status; E-Way Bill Register and Status.
- General Ledger; Party Ledger; Trial Balance; Day Book; Cash Book; Bank Book;
  Profit & Loss; Balance Sheet; Journal Voucher Register.
- Find Cheque Details; Load / Dispatch Register; Load Settlement Summary.
- Product / Item List; Product Price List; Batch and Expiry Master List;
  Customer / Party List; Supplier List; Company List; Area List; Salesman List;
  Godown List; Inactive / Blocked Master Records.
- Permission Audit Report; Audit Trail Report; Dashboard Reconciliation.

NOT IMPLEMENTED AS SEPARATE REPORTS
- Sales: Slow-Moving / Non-Moving Products; Sales Margin / Gross Profit by
  Product; Quotation Register and Pending Quotation.
- Purchase: Purchase Discount and Tax Analysis; Purchase Order Register;
  Pending Purchase Order; Goods Receipt Register.
- Inventory: Overstock; Physical Stock Count / Stock Variance; Godown Transfer;
  Batch Traceability / Batch Movement; Reserved / Committed Stock; Opening
  Stock Register.
- Receivables/payables: Customer Credit Limit Utilization as a separate report;
  Supplier Ageing; Supplier Advance / Unadjusted Payment; Payment Allocation
  and Unallocated Payment. Credit limits appear in Credit Control, but that is
  not a separate utilization report.
- GST/tax: HSN / SAC Summary; GSTR-2A / GSTR-2B Reconciliation; ITC Summary and
  Reconciliation; Tax Rate Wise Sales and Purchase Summary; GST Credit Note and
  Debit Note Reconciliation.
- Accounting: Cash Flow Statement; Contra Voucher Register; Expense Summary /
  Expense Ledger; Cost Centre / Department; Budget vs Actual; Fixed Asset
  Register and Depreciation.
- Banking: Cheque Issue / Receipt Register; PDC Pending / Due / Cleared; Cheque
  Bounce; Bank Reconciliation / Unreconciled Entries; Bank Deposit Register.
- Distribution: Load Wise Invoice and Product Details; Pending Delivery /
  Undelivered Invoice; Shortage, Damage, and Return During Delivery; Load
  Transfer; Delivery Performance by Route / Salesman.
- Administration: Data Import Results / Rejected Rows.

The unimplemented reports require additional data models/workflows or a distinct
calculation path. No report is marked live-verified by this status list.
